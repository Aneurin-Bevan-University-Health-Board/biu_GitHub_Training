# Participants: Your Area

Each person on the training day builds **their own area** in this repository, session by session. By the end of the day you will have used every feature you were taught on real work of your own.

Your pack of crib sheet, checklist, demos and notes template is in [`Participant_Pack/`](../Participant_Pack/README.md).

## The rules

- Your area is your own folder, `participants/<your-folder>/`, named **firstname-lastname** (for example `participants/jerin-george/`). It has been created for you. Only edit inside it.
- Work on your own branch: `training/<your-folder>`. Never commit directly to `main`.
- **Synthetic data only.** No real patient data, no secrets, no tokens, in any file, issue, comment or Copilot prompt.
- Keep your own `.gitignore` in your folder so data and outputs never get committed.
- If you get stuck, ask the trainer. A mistake in your own branch costs nothing; that is the point.

## What your area looks like at the end

```text
participants/<your-folder>/
├── README.md              # who you are and what is in here (Sessions 2 and 4); pre-created
├── .gitignore             # your own ignore rules (Session 3)
├── notes.md               # added in your Codespace (Session 5)
├── starter.ipynb          # a clean notebook (Session 6)
├── referrals.sql          # your SQL for the academy dataset (Session 6)
├── requirements.txt       # pinned dependencies (Session 7)
└── dna-analysis/          # the Copilot exercise (Session 8)
```

## Copy-and-go template

`_template/` holds a starter `README.md` and `.gitignore`. Your folder already has a `README.md`; in Session 3 you copy `_template/.gitignore` into your folder and add a pattern of your own.

## Trainer set-up (before the day)

- Folders are pre-created for the course roster, named firstname-lastname. Add a folder for anyone who joins late by copying `_template/` and keeping only `README.md`. Keep the roster itself (names with email addresses) outside this repository.

- Prefer a **sandbox copy** of this repository so nothing a learner does can affect real work.
- Give every participant **write** access (or have them fork), and protect `main` so that changes need a pull request.
- Optional: a CODEOWNERS rule per participant folder so each person reviews their own area.
